# Third-party software and scientific methods

DistOptLab's own code is provided under the MIT license in LICENSE. Dependencies remain subject to their respective licenses and notices; the project license does not replace them. Dependencies are installed from their distributions, not vendored here.

Direct dependencies: [NiceGUI](https://github.com/zauberzeug/nicegui), [Paramiko](https://github.com/paramiko/paramiko), [Plotly.py](https://github.com/plotly/plotly.py), [latex2mathml](https://github.com/roniemartinez/latex2mathml), [Matplotlib](https://github.com/matplotlib/matplotlib). Exact direct and transitive versions are in requirements-lock.txt. Preserve their applicable license notices when redistributing dependency packages.

Scientific examples implement mathematical methods; software availability does not replace citation of the original research:

- RDCF: Pantoja, Obando and Quijano, Distributed optimization with information-constrained population dynamics, Journal of the Franklin Institute, 2019. DOI: 10.1016/j.jfranklin.2018.10.016.
- RCCRD: Obando and Pantoja, A robust population dynamics model with carrying capacity constraints for centrally coordinated distributed optimization, IEEE Access, 2025. DOI: 10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3578157.
- Reference consensus: Freeman, Yang and Lynch, Stability and convergence properties of dynamic average consensus estimators, CDC, 2006. DOI: 10.1109/CDC.2006.377078.

No journal PDFs, conference manuscript or third-party figures are bundled.
